Why do we ask for your email?

Your email is your login username. Your 228th name, number and Discord are things other members can see, so if those were your login, anyone would already have half of what they need to try getting into your account. Your email stays private to you, which makes your account much harder for someone else to get into. We don't share it or send you anything except account emails, like your confirmation link or a password reset.
