[[File:Comic-book.jpg|left|thumb|311x311px|Comic strip starring 228th.]] The 228th Black Skulls Corps is large clone trooper unit with many trooper programs beneath it like ARC, ARF, Commando, Echelon, and etc. Like many other clone units like the 91st Corps & 7th Sky Corps the 228th in its entirety had over 36,000 troopers. The 228th's main troopers (grunts) were as well trained as standard [[starwars:Clone_shadow_trooper/Legends|Shadow Troopers]] and all the different programs above that were even more specialized and elite. For example the 228th's commando program was a lot harder and produced better results then the standard programs of the GAR.

These clones were heavily trained to conduct recon and assassinations that appeared as accidents on CIS political leaders. The 228th didn't train their members with their tactics since the beginning of their clone lives, but instead, hand-picked clones who excelled in combat movement and tactical theory. This was because they valued the thought and theory of combat more than the run and gun tactics clones were often trained to use.

After clones were chosen for joining the 228th, they were trained in the basics in all kinds of different sections of specialization, e.g. Engineering, Medical Knowledge, disarming explosives, hacking, close combat, torture, etc.

=== '''Operation: Backdoor Neglect''' ===
228th Black Skulls Corps Deployment – [[First Battle of Geonosis]] in [[22 BBY]].

22 BBY — Geonosis

The dust of Geonosis never settled. Not during the war.

Orange haze choked the sky, cast in the glow of Separatist artillery and blaster fire that lanced across the dunes. Clones dropped by the thousands across scattered landing zones. And while the primary Republic lines surged forward in open formations, the 228th Black Skulls Corps was ordered to cut through from the opposite side—pushing straight into the outer ridges of a heavily fortified droid encampment.

This wasn’t about taking ground.

This was about showing the enemy who had just joined the war.

From the belly of a rumbling LAAT/i, Flanker stood at the front, braced against the bulkhead, DC-15A rifle gripped tight across his chest. His black Phase I armor was streaked with ash and trench dirt, the 228th’s crimson markings glowing faintly under the gunship’s interior lights. His red visor flickered briefly—reflecting each passing explosion below as they approached the drop.

The Black Skulls behind him were silent. Every helmet carried the same mark. The same steel-jawed focus. The same promise:

They would hit harder and faster than any standard battalion.

And they would not be ignored.

“Two klicks out!” the pilot barked over comms.

“Shadow Company, on me!” Flanker called. “We drop, we strike, we don’t wait for anyone to catch up.”

The side doors flung open mid-descent. Wind howled through the troop bay.

A heartbeat later, the 228th jumped.

—

The ground slammed up fast. Blaster fire lit the haze from every direction as the Black Skulls landed in a rough circle, forming a defensive line behind jagged outcroppings and the wreck of a downed LAAT.

Ring, CT-2405, dropped beside Flanker, firing short, disciplined bursts with his DC-15s. He moved like he’d been doing this for years—even though it was his first warzone.

“Contact left!” one of the heavies shouted.

A wave of B1 battle droids surged over the ridge, their rifles flaring wild. Behind them came heavier models—two AAT tanks rolling forward, shells already charging.

Flanker didn’t hesitate. He stepped from cover, knelt into firing position, and unleashed a stream of fire from his DC-15A, punching through the chestplates of the first three droids before they fully cleared the ridge.

“Shadow—split formation!” he ordered. “East side flanks, west side climbs!”

The squad obeyed instantly, their black and red forms breaking apart and weaving through the dust storm. Red visors flickered in the light like ghostfire—brief, haunting pulses between detonations.

Ring led the eastern push—four troopers at his back. They darted between shallow craters and fallen droids, slipping behind one of the tanks. A well-placed EMP charge from Ring’s hip pack disabled the rear treads. The AAT pitched forward and slid sideways, leaving it open to a coordinated barrage from Z-6 heavies posted on the western bluff.

It exploded in a fireball that lit up the fog for kilometers.

—

The fight escalated.

Droids poured from bunkers embedded into the cliff face—standard infantry, then supers, then spider droids. But the 228th didn’t fall back.

They pushed harder.

Flanker led the charge personally, vaulting over a barricade and shredding two B1s at close range before planting a detpack against a command node. He ducked just as it blew, metal shards flying past his visor.

When he turned back, Ring was there—rifle hot, visor glowing. He’d taken a hit to the shoulder but didn’t slow.

“You’re not bad, corporal,” Flanker said through comms, nodding once.

Ring blinked. Then grinned under the helmet.

“Copy that… sir.”

—

By nightfall, the droid encampment was dust. Shadow Company held the ridge, survivors scanning for any missed power signatures. Gunships rotated above, air cover locked.

Flanker stood at the edge of a scorched cliff, red visor casting a faint light across the bodies of dismantled droids below. The battlefield was littered with shell casings, fallen tech, and the smoldering remains of Separatist resistance.

The 228th had hit first.

They hadn’t waited.

And Geonosis would never forget it.

—

Field Report – Operation: Backdoor Neglect

“The 228th was deployed behind primary Republic lines to breach a fortified Separatist holdout near the Geonosian outer ridge. Shadow Company led the push. Droid lines were dismantled. Primary targets neutralized. No clone casualties. CT-2405 ‘Ring’ displayed exemplary field control and was promoted to corporal.

Let the clankers remember—we are the Skulls in the dark.”

—CC-5733 “Flanker”

=== '''Operation: The Fleet Admiral''' ===
Phase I Era | 228th Black Skulls Corps | Moraband to Naboo to the 91st’s Defense

The sun over Moraband were just beginning to crest when the LAAT touched down.

Wind from the repulsorlifts kicked up red sand across the landing pad, the distant howls of ancient canyons echoing through the base. From the gunship’s hatch stepped a Kaminoan shuttle escorting a single passenger—Fleet Admiral Lynx.

Newly trained, highly ranked, and sharper than the blade sheathed at Flanker’s side.

Marshal Commander Flanker waited at the edge of the pad, helmet in one hand, Phase I armor gleaming with the wear of combat and command. His red visor tracked her closely. He’d read her training records—top of the command simulation, unmatched fleet tactics, and more field instincts than most veterans.

She didn’t just catch his attention.

She earned it.

“Fleet Admiral,” Flanker greeted. “Welcome to Moraband. You’re 228th now.”

Lynx offered a sharp nod. “Pleasure to serve, Commander.”

Behind him, Hog whistled low under his breath. “The boys are gonna fall over each other for her.”

Flanker shot him a glance. “Then keep them in check.”

—

That night, Moraband’s base was quiet.

No drills. No sorties.

Flanker gave Lynx his quarters—he preferred to walk the halls, boots echoing softly through the red-lit corridors. She didn’t question it. The gesture wasn’t romantic… not yet.

They talked.

A lot.

Plans, war, strategy… but also stories from Kamino. Things they missed. Things they wondered about. Eventually, conversation gave way to quiet companionship. And somewhere in the hours before dawn, Flanker made a quiet offer.

“Leave of absence. Naboo. Just for a few days.”

Lynx smiled. “You asking me to run?”

He shook his head. “I’m asking you to walk with me.”

—

Naboo | Two Days Later

It was unlike anything the war had shown them.

Fields. Waterfalls. Stillness.

No orders. No comm static. Just sky and peace.

It didn’t take long.

Beneath a canopy of willow trees, with no witnesses but the stars above, Flanker and Lynx eloped—no ceremony, no rank, just names.

Two soldiers.

One vow.

—

Moraband | One Week Later

The return to base was quiet, but not for long.

An urgent comm ping from orbit.

The 91st Mobile Recon Corps was under siege. Their capital ship was boarded, defenses breached, and Marshal Commander Neyo was calling for backup.

Flanker didn’t hesitate.

The 228th launched immediately—LAATs streaking skyward, the Moccus breaking orbit and cloaking for overwatch.

“I’ll take a squad and go myself, remain here,” Flanker told Lynx. “It’ll be a surgical strike.”

She nodded. “Yes Sir.”

—

Aboard the 91st’s Flagship | Battle in Progress

Droids poured through the corridors like a plague. The first wave hit the lower decks hard—slicing through clone squads and aiming for the reactor.

Flanker and a small team arrived in stealth, cloaked and alone, Phase I armor dark and marked.

They moved through the chaos like phantoms—slipping between shadows, dragging droids into supply rooms, slitting wires and silencing servos. At one choke point, a 91st trooper was pinned behind a sealed door.

Flanker breached through the wall.

Together they pried the doors open. Blaster bolts sang past. Flanker vanished into cloak again, ducking down the left corridor.

Unseen.

Until he wasn’t.

A trio of B1s turned just as he uncloaked—blade drawn.

One’s collarbone cracked under the strike. Another had its skull crushed into a bulkhead. The third tried to raise its weapon—

Too late.

He tripped it, stomped its head into scrap, and moved on.

—

The battle didn’t last much longer.

Flanker cleared deck after deck until resistance faded.

The 91st survivors, with Neyo and his team at the bridge, restored lockdowns and vented the remaining droids. A few troopers were lost. Most were saved.

—

Back on Moraband

The LAAT touched down just before dawn.

Lynx met Flanker at the ramp.

She didn’t salute. Just walked beside him through the halls of their home—quiet, scarred, united.

He didn’t say much.

Didn’t have to.

They both knew what they’d found in each other.

Not just rank.

But something worth surviving for.

—

Mission Report – Operation: The Fleet Admiral

Filed by: Marshal Commander Flanker

• Objective: Respond to distress call from the 91st

• Status: Success

• Casualties: Minimal

• Primary Action: Cloaked insertion, deck-to-deck purge

• Notes: 91st’s internal breach was a feint. Real intel likely stolen prior. Investigate further

=== '''Operation: Nightingale''' ===
228th Black Skulls Corps – Recon Operation
Kashyyyk, 21 BBY

They called it a walk in the park.

To most of the 228th Black Skulls, Nightingale was a simple sweep mission—low resistance, clean terrain, high spirits. A light op compared to the trench charges of Geonosis or the frozen ambushes on Orto Plutonia.

But in war, nothing stays light for long.

Beneath the dense canopy of Kashyyyk’s southern equatorial jungles, the 228th moved like shadows. Their armor shimmered in hues of deep tropical green, the same camo pattern as always, retextured for the wild—sleek, matte, and silent. Adaptive visors glinted crimson as they caught slivers of sun through the trees, their signature glow flickering with movement.

This wasn’t just a patrol.

They were hunting droids.
----Marshal Commander Flanker crouched near the edge of a moss-covered cliff, DC-15A in hand, his red visor scanning a distant ridge line across the valley. A breeze swept the treetops, sending birds scattering through the light fog. Below, a squad of scout droids crept along an old Wookiee rope bridge, mapping terrain and relaying encrypted signals.

“Eyes on,” Flanker muttered over comms. “Transponders match the frigate we tracked coming out of hyperspace two days ago.”

Beside him, ARF troopers sank low into the undergrowth, their pulse rifles prepped. The entire op was built on speed and silence—locate and destroy seventeen scout droids feeding real-time terrain data to an unknown Separatist vessel still cloaked in orbit.

Every second those droids lived, the Republic’s position risked exposure.
----Further north, CT-2405 “Ring” led his own four-man squad through a tangle of roots and vines. Promoted to Sergeant just days earlier for valor on Geonosis, Ring was fast proving why he earned it.

“Hold,” he ordered, raising a hand.

A thin whir cut through the air. A scout droid—light model, single optic—hovered just above the ferns. It rotated once. Twice.

Then its sensor turned.

Ring didn’t hesitate.

He fired a tight burst from his DC-15s. The droid shattered in the air, sparks flying into the trees. Seconds later, a second unit rose to investigate the noise—too late. His squadmate dropped it with a well-placed shot to the core.

“Two down. Moving.”
----Across the AO, similar hits played out in sequence.

One ARF team swept a ridge and collapsed a nest of three. Another used Wookiee vines to swing silently into position above a glade, landing in the brush and ambushing a cluster of scouts hiding beneath camouflage netting.

Each unit marked confirmed kills—working with discipline, precision, and no need for reinforcements.

It was clean. Efficient. Almost too easy.

Until it wasn’t.
----By mid-afternoon, Flanker’s team hit resistance.

Four scout droids regrouped near an uplink antenna—an improvised relay site built from scrap and native wood. As the squad approached, the droids activated a perimeter burst, lighting up the jungle with a pulse that scrambled HUDs and triggered three proximity mines.

Two troopers went down—one with a leg injury, the other caught in the edge of the blast.

“Medic!” Flanker barked, dragging the wounded clone behind a log. His DC-15A came up in a fluid arc, firing controlled bursts toward the antenna while his squad fanned out around him.

In seconds, the jungle erupted—blaster fire and echoing screeches from the damaged droids filled the air.

ARF support struck fast, circling the clearing and lobbing EMP charges that shorted two units instantly. The last two tried to flee—Flanker dropped one with a center-mass shot. The final droid leapt into the trees, jetpack flaring—

—but Ring’s squad was already moving in.

“Target moving west. I see him,” Ring called out.

He sprinted through the underbrush, leapt off a log, and caught the droid mid-air with a clean shot to its repulsorlift system. It crashed hard into the mud. Ring landed beside it, planted his boot on the chassis, and drove his blade through the central processor.

“Seventeen confirmed,” he said. “Clean sweep.”
----By nightfall, the jungle was quiet again.

From a small clearing, the 228th regrouped. Troopers leaned against trees, cleaned gear, and packed up relay spikes. Medics treated the wounded—none critical. It had been a textbook op with real results.

One by one, scout beacons were crushed under boots. The enemy uplink was slagged.

Over the comms, Lynx relayed from orbit:

“Frigate’s signal went dark. They’ve gone quiet. They know we’re here—and they know we saw them.”

Marshal Commander Flanker stood over the map table, arms crossed.

“We’ll find them next.”

He turned toward Ring, who stood at ease, helmet clipped to his belt.

“You led your squad well. Six droids neutralized, zero injuries.”

“Just following the brief, sir,” Ring replied.

Flanker stepped forward and extended a hand.

“You’re a Sergeant now. Make it count.”

Ring took the handshake, nodding once.

“Yes sir.”
----Mission Debrief – Operation: Nightingale

Field Report: Marshal Commander Flanker

• Operation complete.

• 17 CIS scout droids destroyed.

• Transmission relay neutralized.

• Frigate contact broke signal post-engagement.

• Casualties: 2 wounded, stable.

• Sergeant Ring led successful flanking squad—6 targets eliminated.

• Jungle armor performed above spec in terrain ops. Recommend increased use for recon units in tropical environments.

“Let them listen. Let them watch. When they send shadows, we send fire.”

—CC-5733 “Flanker”

=== '''Operation: Midnight Recluse''' ===

Trees towered like blackened pillars, their leaves rustling in a faint wind. No cities. No noise. Just nature—and the enemy. A LAAT/i skimmed low through the treetops, its lights cut, hull darkened. Inside, four clone troopers stood silent in the red glow of the troop bay. Jungle armor—standard 228th camo but in tropic green—covered them from head to boot, visors tinted in faint crimson. No custom gear. No markings yet. They were just soldiers tonight. Marshal Commander CC-5733 “Flanker” stood at the head of the group, DC-15A slung across his chest. CT-7592 “Hog”—cool and calm, his grip steady on a compact DC-15S. His eyes, behind the visor, tracked every movement with surgeon’s focus. Next to him, his brother from the same training squad—CT-2882 “Nightfang.” A little less certain, a little more cautious, but steady on his boots. He carried the same blaster model as Hog, movements sharp, trained. The LAAT dropped low. Its side doors opened. “Drop zone,” came the pilot’s voice. “No chatter past this point.” Flanker turned to the squad. “We’re not here to be heard. Move quiet. Stay sharp. Targets are flesh and metal—handle both accordingly.” They nodded once. Then jumped. — Their boots hit moss. The jungle swallowed them whole. Not a word was spoken as they moved in formation—Flanker and Ring up front, Hog and Nightfang falling into flank. Scanners mapped the terrain in muted pulses. The compound was just over three klicks away—hidden within the cliffside under a canopy of moss and vines. Inside that facility was a senator. Name redacted. Role confirmed. He had been feeding CIS engineers specialized parts for something massive. A prototype cannon. Capable of devastating moons in two shots. And the 228th wasn’t here to debate politics. They were here to end a betrayal. — The first outpost came into view after forty minutes of silent movement. Four B1s. Two patrols. One turret. “Split,” Flanker whispered. Ring and Hog circled left through the underbrush, while Flanker and Nightfang moved right. One B1 trooper paused, its servos twitching at the sound of wind. Then Flanker was behind it. Nightfang crept up behind another, kicked in its knee joint from the side. The leg bent wrong, and before it hit the ground, his blade slipped through its cranial port—short and clean. Hog knelt beside the turret’s power node, spliced a charge into the control panel. “Turret’s blind,” he whispered. Ring took the last droid with a snap of the neck and a hard twist, dragging the remains into the brush. No alarms. No screams. They advanced. — Inside the perimeter fence, the compound waited like a sleeping beast. The exterior was lit with soft ambient glowpanels. No floodlights. No posted guards. But motion scanners blinked under the moss, and aerial surveillance relayed heat signatures inside the walls. “CIS officers. Four,” Flanker said, checking his datapad. “Senator’s in the central spire. Minimal guards. No civvies. We’re green.” They moved in under cover of branches and shadow, scaling the wall with magclamps and vaulting over the top. A sentry walked the upper platform—barely had time to gasp before Ring kicked in his leg, dropped him to a knee, and slit his throat in one clean stroke. The body slumped silently. The squad fanned out. — Inside the compound, the kill count rose without a word. Nightfang crept behind a technician adjusting power lines—took him by the collar and slammed him into the wall with a stun blade through the chest. Hog took out a comms officer with a crack to the back of the skull, then braced him against the wall like he was still standing. Another trooper passed by and never noticed. Ring disabled a control room with a detpack—timed for five minutes after exfil. Flanker slipped through the halls like a shadow, cutting down B1s and officers alike. One tried to raise a blaster—Flanker swept his leg out, dropped him hard, and drove a vibroblade through his throat. They reached the central spire. — The senator was pacing. Alone. No guards. The door opened. Flanker stepped in first, rifle raised. “Don’t move.” The man froze. “What’s this? You’re not—this is a Republic violation—!” “Cut the feed,” Flanker said. Ring disabled the senator’s comms. Nightfang bound his wrists. Hog scanned the room for data drives. The senator struggled once. “You can’t do this. I have diplomatic—” “You don’t,” Flanker said coldly. “Not anymore.” — The exfil wasn’t clean. A patrol found the bodies. The alarm blared once—then was silenced by a burst of blaster fire from Ring. But the call went out. Droids flooded the compound. Flanker and his squad fought their way through corridors of steel and smoke—cutting down B1s, gutting commando units with knives and short bursts. Ring was the first hit—shrapnel through the thigh from a fragmentation charge. He kept firing, dragging the senator with one hand while laying down cover with the other. “Hog! Get him to the LZ!” Flanker ordered. Hog nodded. “Nightfang, flank with me!” The two cleared the path—shields up, blasters barking. Flanker dropped the last two droids with shots through their oculars and carried Ring on his shoulder for the final run. The LAAT roared in from the darkness. They climbed aboard with seconds to spare. — Back aboard the Re-claimer, the senator was locked in a cell. The mission was logged. 228th casualties: heavy across the compound. Multiple squads didn’t make it. But the core objective was complete. CT-2405 “Ring” was stabilized and transported to a med station in the Bespin system. CT-7592 “Hog” and CT-2882 “Nightfang” had passed their trial by fire. They didn’t say much. Just cleaned their gear, reset their armor. Custom paint would come later. Tonight, they earned the right to wear it. — Mission Log: Operation Midnight Recluse “Objective complete. Senator captured. Prototype supply lines cut. Compound burned. Ring wounded, stable. When the shadows close in, we strike quiet, and we strike clean.” —Marshal Commander Flanker, 228th Black Skulls Corps

=== '''Operation: Leviathan Wall''' ===
Operation: Leviathan Wall 228th Black Skulls Corps – Kamino Defense Protocol 22 BBY – Tipoca City, Kamino The ocean boiled beneath the storm. Black thunderclouds cracked with lightning, reflecting fire across the watery surface. Separatist landing craft broke through the rain like falling daggers, columns of droid pods slicing toward the cloning capital of the Republic. The Battle of Kamino had begun. While many units were deployed to defend cadet barracks, medical wings, and the central cloning vats, the 228th Black Skulls Corps was given a different task: hold the outer western bridge and prevent breach of the southern genetic archives. This wasn’t about survival. It was about denying the enemy every inch of Kaminoan ground. ⸻ Flanker stood in full Phase I armor, the matte black plates of the Black Skulls streaked with crimson lightning. His visor burned red against the white floodlights overhead. By his side, Fleet Admiral Lynx, no longer at the helm of a cruiser, but clad in sleek combat armor—black camo, reinforced plates, twin sidearms holstered at her hips. They weren’t in a command post. They were on the platform. Directly ahead, the battle raged. LAAT/i gunships zipped between towers, some spiraling into the ocean trailing smoke, others holding formation to drop squads of 228th infantry onto slick Kaminoan walkways. V-Wings and ARC-170s streaked overhead, weaving between aerial droid fighters, their cannon bursts illuminating the rain like strobe fire. “Landing craft coming in low!” Lynx barked over comms. “They’re skipping the main lanes—trying to touch down on secondary spires!” “Not happening,” Flanker growled, pointing forward. “228th—on me! === '''Operation: Headhunter''' ===
228th Black Skulls Corps | Planetary Assault – 21 BBY

Commanding Officer: Commander Tank

Mission Designation: Heavy Assault | Full Armor Engagement

The ground shook before the sky caught fire.

Fields of ash and cracked earth stretched across the horizon—scars left by orbital bombardments that softened the Separatist perimeter. Smoke curled into the blood-red sky. Static crackled across the comms.

Then came the rumble.

Turbo Tanks. Dozens of them. Lined front to back, inching across the battlefield like titans. Their armor was scorched, repainted with 228th markings and heavy upgrades—RX-200 laser turrets mounted to their upper decks, mid-size cannons reinforced for rapid assault. Behind them, a spread of TX-130s crawled forward. Each tank bore the black-red paint of the Black Skulls, gleaming through the dust and blood mist.

Commander Tank assigned by Marshal Commander Flanker for this operation; stood atop the forward platform of the lead Juggernaut, visor locked on the Separatist stronghold ahead. The enemy base—half buried in a canyon trench—was pouring out droids by the dozen. Sniper bolts zipped through the air, already picking off exposed clones.

Tank clenched a fist and barked through the battlenet.

“Form the line! Turbo Tanks, forward—tighten formation but maintain blast spacing! TX-130s—rear guard and push cover! AT-TEs, lock elevation and fire into the gaps!”

He turned, pointing toward the west ridge. “All infantry! Use the tanks as mobile cover! Stay low, move tight, and for kriff’s sake—if you see one of ours go down, you drag them back!”

The cannons fired.

The first volley from the Juggernauts shattered the enemy bunkers—blaster emplacements turned to slag, Yavin 4-made walls reduced to craters. Droids launched into the air in pieces—torsos spinning, limbs dismembered in red-orange bursts.

Tank’s boots pounded the hull as he made his way to the rear hatch. His voice came again through comms.

“BARC squad, move wide. Flank fast.”

BARC troopers mounted their speeders and peeled off in formation—black and gray camo blurs streaking through the chaos. They rushed the right flank, weaving between plasma bolts, dropping thermal detonators as they passed. Behind them, the AT-TEs began to lay siege fire in layered arcs, artillery shells thundering through smoke.

Then came the charge.

Tank’s foot soldiers pushed hard behind the tanks—DC-15s firing in bursts, Z-6s spinning up and mowing down droid lines. One clone took a bolt through the chest—his armor cracked, black smoke pouring from the wound.

Recovery Team Delta, cloaked and silent, appeared within seconds. They dragged him back—stabilizing his vitals, hauling his body into a drop pod labeled “Burial-Alpha.”

Another trooper wasn’t as lucky. An AAT round clipped a fuel pack—sending him into the air, screaming, before detonating mid-flight. There was nothing left but scorched fragments.

Tank watched it all unfold, teeth clenched.

“Push harder!” he barked. “We finish this now!”

At the front line, BARC troopers broke into the encampment perimeter. Rockets fired. Mortars launched from their belts. The stronghold erupted in flame and smoke. Droids exploded like shrapnel-filled dolls—some caught mid-run, others bursting from inside as molten steel tore through their chassis.

The screams of CIS officers echoed beneath the barrage—cut short by rubble and fire.

Commander Tank leapt from the lead Juggernaut and sprinted toward the trench mouth. His DC-15A was already up—he fired point blank into a group of five retreating technicians. The last one’s face was caved in by the butt of his rifle.

He kicked open a bunker door.

Inside were three droids and a commander. One droid lifted its weapon—Tank shot through its head, then threw a detpack at the other two. The charge exploded, vaporizing them. The commander stumbled forward, bleeding.

Tank grabbed him by the collar.

“This war’s got no room for cowards.”

Then he dropped him—dead weight—and stepped out of the bunker as flames devoured it behind him.

—

Within hours, the battlefield was a graveyard of metal and smoke.

What droids remained tried to surrender.

They were answered with silence—and a firing line.

The 228th didn’t take prisoners today.

Only vengeance.

—

=== '''Operation: A Change in Plans''' ===
Naboo | 21 BBY
228th Black Skulls Corps – Solo Scout Mission

CT-5537 “Boxer”

The rain had stopped falling hours ago.

Now it was just damp stone and the metallic scent of blood in the air. Somewhere far off, a tortured scream echoed, followed by the slow hum of electrostaffs powering down.

Boxer sat in his cell, breathing slow and shallow.

The 228th had sent him on a recon sweep—standard ops. In, scan the comms relay, out before the droids knew what hit them. But the Clankers were waiting. Commando droids. New models. Too fast. Too precise. He managed to down two before the others took him alive.

He’d been here for four days now.

Long enough to hear the truth.

The prison wasn’t a military outpost. It was a holding facility for civilians—families that had defied the Separatists in quiet ways: withholding food, sheltering clones, refusing to bow. The Warden was no machine. Flesh and blood. A pale-skinned brute with half his face burnt from acid, and the rest twisted with arrogance. He didn’t just allow the torture. He ran it like a sport.

Boxer wasn’t going to die in this place.

And neither was anyone else.

—

Day 2

He watched the guards. Memorized shift patterns. Noted where the cameras twitched, where the feed glitched, where the prisoners screamed loudest.

At dinner, he palmed a spoon.

By lights out, he was on his bunk, blood trickling down his chin.

He jammed the spoon into his upper gums, wiggled.

The tooth gave way—root and all.

A gift from the 228th’s experimental division: a pressure-activated cloaking mod built into a fake molar. Still in testing. Still unapproved.

But he was done waiting.

With a muffled grunt, he stabbed the sharp end of the tooth under his skin where a small hidden port was.

—

Cloak: Active

Boxer faded from view.

One of the guards turned the corner and noticed the empty bed.

He rushed inside.

Boxer slipped behind him, grabbed the back of his helmet, and twisted until the neck snapped with a wet pop. The body hit the floor silently. He moved, invisible, past checkpoints and into the secured vault where prisoner gear was kept.

He suited up in his armor and grabbed his blades.

He picked up a vibroknife, slid it into his wrist sheath, and made his way to the Warden’s quarters.

—

The door hissed open.

The Warden turned from his desk, surprised but not afraid.

Until Boxer uncloaked.

“Who—” the Warden began.

Boxer grabbed him by the throat, lifted him off the ground, and slammed him into the wall. The room shook.

“You killed children,” Boxer said.

The Warden struggled, gasping. “Just… following orders.”

“Me too.”

He stabbed the vibroknife through the side of the Warden’s neck. The blade sliced through arteries and embedded in bone. The Warden gurgled, hands clawing at Boxer’s armor.

Boxer pulled the knife out slowly.

Then, with practiced cruelty, he rammed it through the Warden’s palm, pinning it to the desk. The man screamed.

Boxer removed the blood-slick spoon from his belt—the same one he’d used to tear out his own tooth.

He stabbed the other hand with it.

Then leaned in, voice low and steady:

“You’re going to die for every cry I heard in this place.”

—

He pressed the cell release button. Alarms screamed. Prison doors slid open across the compound. Prisoners stumbled out, confused. Terrified.

Free.

Boxer ignited a detpack and walked away, laying charges every ten meters as he moved through the halls. The remaining guards opened fire.

He didn’t give them the chance to reload.

One got a blade through the collarbone and was used as a shield against the others. Another lost a leg to a low sweep, then his throat to a sharp stomp. It wasn’t a firefight. It was an execution.

He reached the exit, turned back one last time, and detonated the charges.

—

The prison vanished in flame.

—

== 228th Onyx Company ==
Onyx Company is a multi-role infantry signals company forming part of the [[228th Black Skulls Corps™|228th]], 1st Steel Battalion. The predominate focus of Onyx Company is to enable communications for missions and set up radio networks under fire. Onyx Company are trained experts in engineering, operating military systems, networks and cyber equipment. The tight knit company has its own engineers logistics and communications experts, providing the command support to commander and their headquarters.

Onyx Company is a one of a kind, being that, most Signal units operate as a regiment or corps, this isn't the case due to the nature of the [[228th Black Skulls Corps™|228th]]. This in turn means that each member has to have at least 2 specializations spanning up to five in some cases. Specializations can range from Communications Operator training in radio and large communication systems, to Electronic Warfare Systems Operator, trained in intercepting and jamming enemy communications. The nature of Onyx company and their diverse role requires them to work closely with Engineering and Artillery units, whilst also providing support to intelligence units.

=== '''Operation Nightingale:''' ===
The company was responsible for almost all the intelligence relayed during Op Nightingale that resulted in the termination of 17 CIS Scout droids and the loss of 3 288th Troopers. Onyx Coy. were not directly involved in the fighting that took place were able to utilize their knowledge of the terrain to carry out their role with ease, preventing around 40% of the enemy intel from being received by the enemy headquarters.

=== '''Operation Servitor:''' ===
Tasked with setting up communications arrays on the then hostile planet of Genosis Onyx Company were amongst the first to get their boots on the ground.

Whilst heavy fighting ensued, most casualties were sustained by other fighting units which provided the much needed support for the 228th to establish a HQ in which to launch operations from. The secondary objective was to relay information via communications about the location of small scale enemy units on and within the planet's atmosphere.

The Echelon's were an elite group of clones within the 228th Black Skulls Corps The lead Squad known as Night-Stalker Squad lead by Flanker and consisted of one other, known as Strider. Flanker and Strider made the decisions of who would be selected for try outs and would decide who would get to be apart of the elite program, they decided that they worked in teams of two, they were clones who showed the most potential, but just because they were picked for the program does not mean they were going succeed there. The 228th's 1st Advanced Recon Force Company [228-1stARF], named Hellhound Company, was an elite sub-unit comprised of [[starwars:Advanced_Recon_Force_trooper|Advanced Recon Force]] Personnel operating under the [[228th Black Skulls Corps™|228th]]. Infamous even within the 228th for their brutality, Hellhound Company predominantly conducted High Risk Reconnaissance missions; dropping in behind enemy lines and infiltrating CIS territory to gather intelligence on troop positions, movements as well as any and all information regarding High Value Targets. Hellhound would also conduct surprise attacks such as ambushes on CIS Patrols to pave way for a larger 228th Assault. What differentiated the Company from others is they forewent the traditional Clone Doctrine of large scale assaults in favor of intense guerilla warfare.   

Hellhound Company would utilize a variety of equipment to ensure mission success, and were trained in a multitude of equipment such as the [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport|AT-RT walker]], [[starwars:RPC-2_Rocket_Launcher|RPC-2 Rocket Launcher]], life-form scanners, the C.S.E.L System, and [[starwars:BARC_speeder|BARC Speeders]]. Each ARF Trooper within Hellhound were skilled marksmen, taking out targets from a distance.

Named in honor of ARF Designation 0666 "Satan", whom originally lead the company, Hellhound Company fully embraced the name, referring to themselves as a "Pack" and their members as "Hounds". Their motto was '''''"Let loose the dogs of war!"'''''. This however caused a stigma, causing members of Hellhound to only follow orders from their own, Pyscho or [[CC-5733 "Flanker"|Flanker]] and turning their cheek to anyone else.

Lead by [[ARF-3925 "Spook"|ARF Designation 3925 "Spook"]] (internally referred to as "The Alpha"),  Hellhound would often work in cooperation with other Companies - especially other elite sub-units such as Phantom Company and [[Ghost Company]] or in the case of major operations other larger battalions such as the  Onyx [[Onyx Company|Battalion]], moving ahead of the main force to provide reconnaissance and pathfinding for drops, convoys and detailed locations and armaments of CIS Forces.

== History ==
[[File:Hellhound Company.png|left|frameless|450x450px|A Hellhound Company photo. ]]

=== The Purge ===
During routine patrols, Hellhound would be one of the first units to spot the CIS forces converging on the 228th's position and relay the warning - preventing more of the 228th's vulnerable forces from being further wiped out. Hellhound also provided cover fire while the wounded, namely Hog, were loaded into the LAAT's.

=== Operation Predator N' Prey ===
Following the attack on the 228th during "The Purge", Hellhound - bloodthirsty for revenge - would conduct intelligence gathering missions to locate CIS Informant Koshak Biggens. The Company was then tasked with clearing a landing zone for the main forces for the commencement of the operation.

=== Operation Bloodlust. ===
''"We're operating out of convention here, Sir."'' Spook pointed out. His scoped DC-15X rested on his knees as he stared through his scope. 

''"That may be well and true,"'' Responded now 2LT Satan, nudging his friend as they gazed down the hill at the Separatist forces. ''"But when did we ever play by the rules?"'' 

''"I'll drink to that one. Here come's the convoy."'' 

Operation Bloodlust was a secretive operation conducted by Hellhound Company in retaliation for The Purge and the death of Hog. 2 weeks before Operation Homebase, The Company scouted out CIS Positions of an Officer believed to have had a hand in the attack - assaulting a small Starport controlled by the CIS where the Officer was believed to be. However, of the group, one wouldn't be coming home. During the fighting, 2LT Satan was mortally wounded by an exploding detonator, and dragged into cover by Spook. The leader of the Company had to be Medevac'd back to base leaving Spook to continue leading the assault. Enraged by the injury of his friend and mentor, Spook fired his blaster point blank into the officer rather than keeping him alive.. Upon arriving back to the Venator, Spook learned news of Satan's passing, leaving him in Command of Hellhound. Spook looked into the mirror, and saw the Bloodstains on his mask resembling that of a smile - to which he kept as a permanent paintjob in memory of Satan.

=== Operation Dirt & Grime ===
During the 228th's mobilization to an immensely unstable region known as "Madrigal", Hellhound Company would be deployed much earlier than the bulk of the force; providing valuable intel and reconnaissance of the area. Upon landing, Hellhound would establish Combat Outpost Fang from which to conduct operations. For a month, the Company would scout out the local CIF forces and concentrations. However, just before the rest of 228th were scheduled to link up with Hellhound COP Fang came under heavy siege from nearby CIS forces, launching waves of droids at the COP and barraging the defenses with mortars. Hellhound fought off the attackers as Spook radioed back to the Venator for reinforcements, sniping them from whatever covered positions they could manage among the rubble before [[CC-5733 "Flanker"|Flanker]] arrived to help secure the area. Once the siege had been won, Hellhound moved in to attack the local CIS Intelligence Outpost with the rest of the 228th, helping to establish Forward Operations Base Hunter.

== Organization ==

=== "Packs" and Specialization ===
Hellhound Company was organized into "Packs" - Squads of up to 6 members, usually consisting of 1 [[Clone Trooper Medic|Medic]], 1 Anti-Tank Specialist, and 1 [[Clone Engineer|Engineer]]. Unlike other companies, Hellhound did not have dedicated roles for snipers or marksmen; Each member was in their own right a skilled marksman. While uncommon, Hellhound Packs could be attached to other units for the purpose of assisted Recon. But more often than not, Hellhound would operate together as one.  

Alpha Pack was Spook's personal Squad within Hellhound that operated as one of the 228th's "Black Operations" Squads. Tasked with much darker and obscure missions that were always Top Secret and highly classified. Various missions Alpha Pack have been tasked with are; Tracking down and "Fetching" 228th deserters, Framing the CIS for attacks on Neutral Territories, among others. Units that Alpha Pack has worked with outside of the 228th include [[Covert Ops Clone Trooper|Covert Ops]], [[Clone commando|Republic Commandos]], and [[Clone Assassin|Clone Assassins]].  

=== Weapon Systems ===
Hellhound Company primarily utilized the [[starwars:DC-15x_sniper_rifle/Legends|DC-15X]] Marksman rifle modified to be silenced with a scope with multiple zoom settings. For Close Quarters, Hellhound members utilized the [[DC-17 Hand Blaster|DC-17]] Pistol, with Officers dual wielding. Anti-Tank Specialists utilized the [[starwars:RPC-2_Rocket_Launcher|RPC-2 Rocket Launcher]].  Hellhound members would also utilized the 228th's modified DC-15A and DC-15S weapons systems.

== Order 66 ==
After Order 66 was issued, Hellhound Company like the rest of the 228th executed the order without Hesitation, though failing to kill Jedi General Ghost.  Hellhound would be deployed to the Jedi Temple to hunt down any escapees before they managed to get off world. 

Following Order 66, Hellhound would be updated from an ARF Unit into the 228th's Shadow Scout unit - though opting to keep their 228th ARF armor - becoming referred to as "The Rebel Hunters". Once Flanker found Lynx again and ordered his men to defect from the Empire, Hellhound would follow Spook with the rest of the 228th and become more of a Mercenary Force. Working together to survive as brothers and a unit.

ARF Designation CC-3925, nicknamed "Spook" was an elite ARF Trooper and Clone Major assigned to the [[228th Black Skulls Corps™|228th Black Skulls Corps]] and leader of [[Hellhound Company]] following the death of [[Satan]].  

Born as CT Designation 3925, he would eventually become recognized during his training at Kamino for his inept talent for Stealth which earned him the nickname Spook.  It was due to this that Spook would be selected to receive advanced training under the Advanced Recon Force program, and would be noticed by - at the time - [[CC-5733 "Flanker"|Captain Flanker]] of the [[228th Black Skulls Corps™|228th]] who extended an invitation to the battalion.  

Joining the [[228th Black Skulls Corps™|228th]], Spook would undergo more extensive training, becoming one of the first members of [[Hellhound Company|Hellhound]] - a unit lead by [[ARF-0666 "Satan"]] before his passing to which leadership would be handed down to Spook.  

After his actions in Operation Bloodlust, Spook would be sent back to Kamino to undergo training under the Advanced Recon Commando (ARC) Program. However, after completing this course he opted to remain as an ARF Captain, while keeping his ARC Certifications and the Jaig Eyes he earned.  

Upon returning to the 228th, he would continue to lead Hellhound as the "Alpha Hound" throughout various missions, before they were called back to Geonosis for the Second Battle.   

Spook continued to lead the unit as one of the most feared of the republic, rising to the rank of Captain within the 228th and the 1st Systems Army - helping to lead the 228th during major operations, and continuing to command and manage Hellhound Company. 

Spook himself would also become notorious within the Republic, and the small circle that knew of the 228th. A brutal hunter, and skilled leader. He cared for his Hounds, his men. He lost friends to the war, and would not show mercy for those that slain the only family he knew.  

== Description ==
Spook was on the taller side of Clones, having a whopping height of 2.08 Meters. Minor mutations in his genetics caused the Iris of his eye to take on a Crimson Color. The markings on the forehead of his helmet are a simplified variant of Jaig Eyes.  

His equipment included a Westar-M5 Blaster Rifle, DC-15X Marksman Rifle, dual DC-17's as well as a GNK Power Droid named "Spook Jr". He was also equipped with the 228th's Cloaking System. 

The small fang-like details below his glowing visor are a Symbol of his Grief in losing his brothers and his mentor Satan.

CT-1199 or "Halves" is a corporal in the 228th Black Skulls Corp. His nickname "Halves", comes from his serial number 1199. He also has a personalized armor pattern which he designed due to his given nickname to give a halve and halve effect on his armor. He also decorated his helmet by adding a tally for each time he ''"didn't die", ''and adding a human pin-up girl next to the tallies.

== Biography ==

=== Corporal ===
CT-1199 earned the nickname "Halves" towards the beginning of his life, this was due to his cereal number being 1199. At first, Halves didn't really like the nickname, he preferred the anonymity. Being just another soldier is who he wanted to be, but eventually, the name grew on him and towards the end of his initial training he was handpicked to join the [https://clonewarsfannon.fandom.com/wiki/228th_Black_Skulls_Corp%E2%84%A2 228th Black Skulls Corp] after showing that he could put immense thought into battle in rapid succession and always have a backup plan for when scenarios turned bad. Upon joining the [http://clonewarsfannon.wikia.com/wiki/228th_Black_Ops 228th], Halves was required to take on an extra year of training and conditioning, since he wasn't born or made for the role it requires a lot of training to learn these new skills and techniques. Once his training was complete, Halves was enrolled as a Corporal due to his prowess and combat ability and was eventually shipped off with his squad mates to [[Kashyyyk]] to take part in their first mission (Operation Nightingale). The mission was an overall success with the [http://clonewarsfannon.wikia.com/wiki/228th_Black_Ops 228th] taking very minimal casualties. During his journey to his next mission on Endor, Halves altered his once almost fully camouflaged armor to a half and half look and later adding a pin-up girl to his helmet painting tally decals onto his helmet. This made Halves stand out in his battalion and soon the entire battalion began to learn who halves was, and what he was capable of on the field. 

=== Operation Nightingale ===
Operation Nightingale took place on [[Kashyyyk]], the primary objective was to track and shut down CIS scouts that had infiltrated the planet's defenses who were relaying intel back to an unknown CIS frigate. Halves was second in command of his squad and led his own fireteam within that squad. His team was responsible for pushing hard towards an enemy combatant whilst his squadmates kept him covered. During the operation Halves and his squad had already taken out an enemy scout before they received their brief on the planet. The mission was considered a success with Halves squad taking no casualties yet hunting and shutting down over 17 droid scouts on the planet.

=== Operation Midnight Recluse ===
Operation Midnight Recluse was a 4 hour night mission that was launched at Endor's midnight. The offensive's primary objective was to capture an unnamed senator who was supplying the CIS with parts to build a super cannon, capable of destroying moons and planets in a matter of shots. Only 1 company was dropped in, holding the other 2 back in reserve. Halves was a member of the company that was dropped onto Endor and had to take charge of his squad after his sergeant was temporarily taken out during the first firefight. Although the mission was written down as a success many member of the 228th will always remember the mission for it's heavy losses taken in just a few hours. The members of the 228th used this operation as a lesson and grew from it and decided to become better.

== Equipment ==
Corporal Halves wore standard phase II armor, with a modified visor for better vision during night ops. He also carries a DC-15a into battle, occasionally modified to keep the blaster sounds quieter than usual.

* The first design included grey armor and plain black armor on each half, but the alternate head design was created by accident and to stay with the 228th theme the camo was applied.
