'''Onyx Company''' is a multi-role infantry signals company forming part of the [[228th Black Skulls Corps™|228th]], 1st Steel Battalion. The predominant focus of Onyx Company is to enable communications for missions and set up radio networks under fire. Onyx Company are trained experts in engineering, operating military systems, networks and cyber equipment. The tight knit company has its own engineers  logistics and communications experts, providing the command support to commander and their headquarters.

Onyx Company is a one of a kind, being that, most Signal units operate as a regiment or corps, this isn't the case due to the nature of the [[228th Black Skulls Corps™|228th]]. This in turn means that each member has to have at least 2 specialisations spanning up to five in some cases. Specialisations can range from Communications Operator training in radio and large communication systems, to Electronic Warfare Systems Operator, trained in intercepting and jamming enemy communications. The nature of Onyx company and their diverse role requires them to work closely with [[Engineer|Engineering]] and [[Artillery]] units, whilst also providing support to intelligence units.

== History ==
[[File:IMG 3468.JPG|left|thumb|182x182px]]

=== Operation Nightingale ===
The company was responsible for almost all the intelligence relayed during Op Nightingale that resulted in the termination of 17 [[CIS]] Scout droids and the loss of 3 288th Troopers. Onyx Coy. were not directly involved in the fighting that took place and were able to utilise their knowledge of the terrain to carry out their role with ease, preventing around 40% of the enemy intel from being recieved by the enemy headquarters. 
=== Operation Servitor ===
Tasked with setting up communications arrays on the then hostile planet of Geonosis Onyx Company were amongst the first to get their boots on the ground. 

Whilst heavy fighting ensued, most casualties were sustained by other fighting units which provided the much needed support for the 228th to establish a HQ in which to launch operations from. The secondary objective was to relay information via communications about the location of small scale enemy units on and within the planet's atmosphere.

== Organisation ==

=== Ranks and Roles ===
Unlike the majority of units in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] Onyx Company required their own rank structure. Traditionally Lieutenants lead a company sized unit, but given the speciality the troops selected for this role are generally of higher rank. To combat this the Onyx Company uses a unique rank system that follows equivalent ranks in the rest of the army. However the highest rank a member of 'O Coy' can have is still the equivalent of major.
